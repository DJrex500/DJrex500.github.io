I'm a software engineer who got hooked on the moment code turns into something you can actually play. Most of my time goes into building browser-based games from scratch — no engine, just JavaScript, Three.js and a lot of iteration.

Right now I'm deep in B-Shot Royal, a battle royale that runs entirely in the browser. Building it meant writing my own weapon system, building mechanics, loot tables, a closing storm, and AI enemies that actually fight back.

When I'm not shipping features, I'm digging into 3D graphics, game physics, and figuring out how to make multiplayer work over WebSockets.
